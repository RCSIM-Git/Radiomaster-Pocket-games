POCKETMON: DRONE EDITION - EDGETX 128x64 MONOCHROME LCD EDITION
================================================================================

The Ultimate FPV Drone RPG for EdgeTX Transmitters!
Optimized specifically for RadioMaster Pocket & MT12 (128x64 monochrome LCD).

CONCEPT:
Capture, tune, and battle rogue FPV drones in a retro Game Boy RPG world!

STARTERS:
- WHOOPY   (Micro Whoop - Fast, Agile, High RPM)
- 5"BEAST  (Freestyle Monster - Heavy, 6S Power, High Durability)
- TOOTHY   (Toothpick 2-3" - Ultra Lightweight, High Speed)
- CINEMAX  (Cinewhoop - Armored Ducts, Smooth Roll)

WILD ROGUE QUADS:
- MOBULA 7 (Brushless Micro Whoop)
- NAZGUL 5 (Aggressive 5" Freestyle)
- TOOTHY   (Wild toothpick racer)
- PHANTOM  (Legendary GPS Camera Drone boss!)

BATTLE ACTIONS:
- FIGHT: Real FPV maneuvers (THROTTLE PUNCH, POWER LOOP, PROP CHOP, YAW SPIN,
         TURTLE MODE, BEEPER BLAST, DUST BLOW, RETURN HOME)
- BAG:   BIND PACKET (Send ELRS phrase to bind rogue drone to your Hangar)
         LIPO 4S (Restores +20 mAh/HP)
- HANGAR: Switch active model in your 6-model memory
- DISARM: Emergency disarm & fly away safely

CONTROLS:
- Movement (Overworld): Gimbals / Sticks (AIL/ELE/RUD)
- Menu Navigation: Scroll Roller Wheel
- Select / Confirm / Attack: Press Roller [ENT]
- Cancel / Back / Open Start Menu: Press [RTN]
- Quick Repair / Charge Lipos: Flip Switch [SE]
