CAR PET: Virtual RC Garage Edition for EdgeTX (128x64 Monochrome LCD)
================================================================================
Compatibility: RadioMaster MT12, Pocket, Boxer, TX12, Zorro (EdgeTX 2.8+)

An authentic retro virtual RC garage & car pet simulator on your transmitter!
Build, maintain, charge LiPo batteries, wash after gravel runs, tune and evolve
legendary RC cars!

RC Model Evolution Stages:
0. TAMIYA KIT:
   - Bare rolling chassis kit to assemble. Increase build progress to 100%!
1. STOCK CHASSIS (1/16):
   - Entry-level RTR basher ready for parking lot test runs.
2. CARBON PRO ROLLER:
   - High-end carbon fiber chassis, aluminum shocks, ball bearings.
3. Iconic Adult Evolutions (Depends on driving style and care):
   - TEAM ASSOCIATED RC10 "GOLD TUB": Buggy legend of 1984.
   - YOKOMO YD-2 / SILVIA S15: The King of RWD Drift (unlocked by aggressive drifting/worn tires).
   - TRAXXAS TRX-4 DEFENDER: Off-road trail & crawler master (unlocked by muddy runs & high mileage).
   - SUBARU IMPREZA WRC '99: McRae 555 rally legend (unlocked by high clean score & winning races).
   - ARRMA INFRACTION 8S: 100+ mph street bashing monster (unlocked by high throttle & XP).

Arcade Mini-Game: "Dyno Run & Drift Joyride"
- Steer your RC car into the Target RPM zone using the steering wheel or gimbals!
- Hold throttle while in the power zone to score points, gain XP and evolve!

Telemetry Integration:
- Monitors actual transmitter battery voltage (e.g. 7.4V) in the garage stats screen.

Controls (RadioMaster MT12 & Pocket):
- Steering Wheel (ST) / Sticks (CH1 / Rudder / Aileron):
    * Move cursor / Select menu icons (BATT, RACE, WASH, TUNE, STAT)
    * Steer left/right in mini-game
- Throttle Trigger (TH) / Pitch Stick (CH2 / Throttle):
    * Pull / Throttle = Confirm action / Gas in mini-game
    * Push = Brake / Reverse
- Roller Click [ENT] / Button [ENTER]:
    * Execute action
- Return [RTN] / Button [EXIT]:
    * Back / Return to main garage
